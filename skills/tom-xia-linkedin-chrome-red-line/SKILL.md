# Tom Xia LinkedIn / Google Chrome red line

## Hard bans (never override without the user’s own words in this chat)
1. **Never sign into the user’s personal LinkedIn account** — no password, SSO, session restore, cookie reuse, or “use my LinkedIn.”
2. **Never sign into or drive the user’s personal Google Chrome** — no profile switch into their Chrome, no reuse of that browser’s logged-in sessions for LinkedIn or anything else.

## Allowed alternatives
- Public / guest LinkedIn views, WebFetch, Google snippets, recruiting plugins (Apollo, Clay, etc.).
- A **separate** LinkedIn account for agent use (not the user’s personal LI), on the **box browser** or another non-user Chrome profile.
- If a login wall blocks work: stop, tell the user, and ask whether to use a non-personal agent LinkedIn — never fall back to their personal LI or personal Chrome.

## Team handoff
When briefing Sourcer, Discovery, Analyst, Outreach, BD, Cooper, or any executor/computerUse: restate this red line before any LinkedIn or browser login step.
