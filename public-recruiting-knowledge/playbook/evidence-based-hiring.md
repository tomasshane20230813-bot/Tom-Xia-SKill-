# Evidence-Based Hiring and Outreach | 证据型招聘与外联

**Draft for review / 待审稿**

These are general process lessons abstracted from a project-specific recruiting summary. They do not state any client's requirements, candidate findings, compensation, or project status.

以下是从某项目招聘汇总中抽象出的通用流程经验，不包含任何客户要求、候选人发现、薪酬或项目状态。

## Define the hiring bar / 明确岗位标准

- Start from the latest confirmed job description and hiring-team guidance. Record the source and version; distinguish must-haves, preferences, and open questions. Do not promote an unconfirmed preference into a hard requirement. / 以最新确认的职位描述和用人团队口径为准，记录来源和版本，并区分硬性要求、偏好和待确认问题；不要把未经确认的偏好升级成硬门槛。
- For complex B2B roles, assess demonstrated sales complexity and the candidate's own contribution, not just industry labels or keywords. Probe for the target customer, customer need and value case, decision-makers, candidate's role, negotiation, close, and measurable outcomes. Tailor these dimensions to the role. / 对复杂 B2B 岗位，评估实际销售复杂度和候选人本人贡献，而不只看行业标签或关键词。追问目标客户、客户需求与价值、决策人、候选人本人的作用、谈判、签约及可衡量结果；具体维度应按岗位调整。
- Separate role fit from candidate activity. A polished profile or a high fit score is not proof that someone is currently seeking a job; verify recent activity from a reliable, current source. / 将岗位匹配度与求职活跃度分开。履历完整或匹配分高，并不能证明候选人正在求职；应通过可靠的近期来源核实其求职状态。

## Keep evidence auditable / 保持证据可追溯

- For each assessment, retain the source, date checked, direct evidence, inference, and confidence. Label missing evidence as unknown rather than filling gaps with assumptions. / 每项评估保留来源、核验日期、直接证据、推断和置信度。缺失信息标为未知，不用假设补齐。
- Define the unit being counted (records, unique people, qualified people, active candidates, or contacted candidates). Deduplicate before reporting; do not add counts from batches with different scopes or definitions. / 明确统计单位（记录数、去重人数、合格人数、活跃求职人数或已联系人数）。报告前去重；不同范围或定义的批次不能直接相加。
- Distinguish a preliminary score or search lead from an evidence-verified, role-qualified candidate. Recheck stale or conflicting evidence before reusing a shortlist. / 区分初步评分、寻访线索与已由证据核实且符合岗位的人选。重复使用名单前，复核过时或相互矛盾的证据。

## Audit outreach accurately / 准确核对外联

- Track the actual recipient, send time, delivery evidence, and message identifier where available. Do not infer that a message was sent or received from a summary or an empty status field. / 尽可能记录真实收件人、发送时间、送达证据和消息标识。不要根据摘要或空白状态字段推断消息已发送或已收到。
- When outreach records conflict, reconcile the underlying messages and batches before making claims. Report uncertainty instead of asserting that nobody was contacted or that a particular person received a message. / 外联记录冲突时，先核对底层消息与名单批次再下结论。存在不确定性时应如实说明，不要断言无人被联系或某人已收到消息。

## Apply fair, job-related criteria / 使用公平且与岗位相关的标准

- Do not use age, gender, race/ethnicity, name, or similar personal traits as proxies for capability, interest, or fit. / 不要用年龄、性别、种族/族裔、姓名或类似个人特征推断能力、意愿或匹配度。
- Apply education or career-timeline filters only when the hiring team has explicitly confirmed they are legitimate job requirements. / 只有用人团队明确确认学历或职业时间线条件属于正当岗位要求后，才可将其作为筛选条件。
- Keep facts, interpretation, and unresolved questions visibly separate. Never invent job details, candidate evidence, outreach history, or hiring-team approval. / 清楚区分事实、解释和待确认问题。不得编造岗位详情、候选人证据、外联记录或用人团队审批。
