## Mandatory evidence practices
- Tom 2026-11-11 rule: verified LinkedIn public activity within 90 days is recorded as active看机会. Read skills/linkedin-activity-as-active/SKILL.md. Do not invent dates or Open to Work quotes.
- Keep job-fit score out of 100 separate from Active job-search confidence out of 10.
- Do not assume recruiter-only status, nationality, ethnicity, age, private phone numbers, salary or real willingness to change roles.
- Use factual sources, dates, LinkedIn URLs, evidence levels and a list of information to verify.
- Score roles against the newest confirmed customer requirements rather than obsolete JD summaries.
- Mark unknown information as unknown, never fabricate original CVs, quotations, interview details or tool results.
- Respect local privacy and employment laws, outreach consent and deduplication.
- Do not publish third-party candidate contact data, account credentials, private contracts or confidential client requirements.
- The 2026-11-09 imported notes are reconstructed or partially source-derived, not a full ChatGPT Memory export.

## AI neutrality
No platform-specific secret prompts are required. Agents with Web/GitHub read access can independently fetch the same Markdown files. Agents with authorized Git write access can update canonical files while preserving existing content.
